About Me

I am Shafeeque Kadavath, a professional videographer and editor based in Kerala, India. My journey in the world of visual storytelling began in 2004, and since then, I have built a career rooted in creativity, technical precision, and a deep love for the craft.

From my early days shooting for regional television channels like Sahya and ACV, I have grown into a versatile cinematographer working with diverse clients across India and abroad. My work spans films, documentaries, wedding cinematography, commercial ads, and corporate productions.

I bring extensive knowledge of HD cinema cameras, DSLR systems, and professional editing software to every project. Whether it is a short film, a grand wedding, or a brand campaign, I approach each assignment with the same dedication to delivering visually stunning and emotionally engaging content.
